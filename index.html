Play On Display

A new kind of game

Games where the table plays and your phone controls.

One shared screen, an iPad on the table or a TV on the wall, and everyone plays from the phone in their hand.

Not a board game. Not a card game. Play On Display.

How it plays

The same three beats, round after round.

  1. Glance at the table

    The shared screen shows the game: the board, the cards in play, whose turn it is.

  2. Act on your phone

    Your hand stays private on your phone. Pick a card, buzz in, vote, draw: tap and it's done.

  3. See it on the table

    Your move lands on the big screen for everyone, and the next turn begins.

Why it works

  • One screen does the job of a dozen. An iPad on the table or a TV on the wall.
  • Your phone is your controller. Your hand stays private.
  • The host brings the screen. Everyone else brings a phone or tablet.
  • No boxes, no lost rules, no missing pieces. 215 games, rules built in.
  • No download. Just open the browser and play.
  • Play anywhere: dinner, car rides, tailgates, even Euchre on a windy lake.
  • Zero clutter. Moms love it.
  • Two players or a full room, on devices you already own.

Not every player needs a phoneKids without phones can use a borrowed tablet, like a parent's, or a small 7-inch tablet the family keeps for car rides. The host brings the display (an iPad or a TV), and everyone else brings whatever they have. One catch: a smartwatch won't do it; you need a phone or tablet screen.
